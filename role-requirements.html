/role requirements

Description

A role requirement compares a player or account value against a configured count. During synchronization, all requirements assigned to a role must pass for the member to keep or receive that role. See Role Management for processing order and Role Requirement Type ✏️ for supported values.

Sub-Commands

  • /role requirements get: list requirements for one role.

  • /role requirements list: list every requirement in the server.

  • /role requirements add: create a requirement.

  • /role requirements edit: change a requirement by ID.

  • /role requirements delete: delete a requirement by ID.
